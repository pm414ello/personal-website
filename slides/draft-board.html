The Two-Flex Board

A fantasy football draft tool for a 12-team, two-flex league: my rankings, the room’s ADP and a game plan on one screen.

1

Rank vs. the room

Every player gets my own board rank next to ADP. The gap shows who lasts longer than he is worth (green) and who the room overpays for (red).

2

Rules, not vibes

Seven position-timing rules turn the board into decisions: when to take a TE, when to stop waiting on RBs, defense last.

3

Live during the draft

Click a name to mark him gone, + to roster him. The board, roster counts and next-pick advice update as the draft moves.
